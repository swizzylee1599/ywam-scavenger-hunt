How to Play

Race together, stay safe, and have fun!

  1. Join with your name and province. You will be placed on a team of 4–5.
  2. Complete challenges together and upload the required photo or video.
  3. The organizer reviews every upload. Only approved submissions earn points.
  4. If an upload is rejected, you will see the reason and can submit a new attempt.
  5. The race lasts exactly three hours after the organizer presses Start Hunt.
  6. Stay with your team, respect people and places, follow local laws, and never take unsafe risks.
